# YouTube fitness channels to study

Snapshot taken November 27, 2026. The shortlist is for Workout Flow's YouTube planning for adults 25 and older.

## How the channels qualify

Both cutoffs are strict. A channel needs more than 15,000 subscribers and more than 15,000 mean views across its latest ten public regular uploads. We sampled the YouTube **Videos** tab in latest-first order and excluded Shorts, livestreams, and members-only uploads. The 2× preference compares mean views per upload with current subscribers.

The counts use YouTube's public Videos tab display values for a consistent comparison. The display can round subscriber and view counts. Each upload has had a different amount of time to collect lifetime views, so this is a snapshot of reach across ten uploads. The median and largest upload's share show how representative the mean is. Audience fit comes from the creator's positioning and topics. Public subscriber counts do not show the age of viewers. Liam Fitness is included for its music-led workout format.

## Where to start

- **Fitness app channel.** Runna TV averaged 108,500 views on 53,800 subscribers. Study how it pairs a training-plan episode with a follow-up that shows the actual attempt. Workout Flow could follow someone adapting their workouts to the equipment they have, then show what changed over four sessions. [Plan episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gZxl53Rr3Ag) and [attempt episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vC0WfhCvSJo).
- **Repeatable gym and cardio series.** Liam Fitness had nine uploads above 2× its subscriber count, and Teagan Kirin had seven. SafalFitPro still averages 2.76× after removing its highest-view upload. Compare numbered dance workouts, lifting milestones, and walking step goals. [Liam Fitness](https://www.youtube.com/@Liam_reality_1/videos), [Teagan Kirin](https://www.youtube.com/@TeaganKirin/videos), [SafalFitPro](https://www.youtube.com/@safalfitpro/videos).
- **Breakout how-to topics.** The top upload accounts for 51% of Oscar Moves' sample, 69% of GYM decoded's, and 65% of Jacob Juul Hansen's. Study which problem or question made the breakout approachable. Oscar's 44.5K median gives a better sense of a typical upload than its 746K mean. [Oscar Moves](https://www.youtube.com/@OscarMovesYt/videos), [GYM decoded](https://www.youtube.com/@GYMDECODED1/videos), [Jacob Juul Hansen](https://www.youtube.com/@jacobcalisthenics/videos).
- **Training around adult schedules.** Kettlebell Quickies has a 33K median on 23.4K subscribers, and its top upload accounts for just 18% of sampled views. Sean Alami has a stronger age-specific hook with his 40+ home-strength videos. Study titles that tell a busy viewer the equipment, time, and goal. [Kettlebell Quickies](https://www.youtube.com/@KettlebellQuickies/videos) and [Sean Alami](https://www.youtube.com/@SeanAlami/videos).

- **Neon** found four channels that cleared 2×. Its strongest contribution was finding examples across mobility, beginner gym education, and app-led running. Runna TV is the closest business model. Kettlebell Quickies had a higher typical view count relative to subscribers than several of Neon's 2× picks.
- **Opus** found ten channels that cleared 2×. Its recent high-view searches surfaced Liam Fitness and SafalFitPro, the strongest cardio results. James Li's teen-focused titles and The Iron Historian's bodybuilding documentaries made them weaker fits for the adult training shortlist.
- **Codex** found two channels that cleared 2× and prioritized coverage across formats. It found Teagan Kirin, the strongest lifting series in the shortlist, and Mica Rivera Wood, a reference for documenting a training block and race. It also identified follow-along options for home strength and cardio.

Jacob Juul Hansen was the only duplicate among the top tens, appearing in both Neon and Codex. Opus also found Mica Rivera Wood and Will Tennyson as runners-up. Both made the Codex top ten. David and Megan Roche made the Neon top ten and were a runner-up for Opus. The final selection retains five channels below 2× for adult positioning, steadier median performance, or topic coverage.
